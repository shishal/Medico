What you can do

The loop that actually prepares you

Browse a subject, open a lesson, practise PYQs, then drill MCQs on the topics you marked weak — with a tracker for the next internal.

01

KUHS PYQ bank

Theory questions tagged by year, paper, and how often they repeat. Optional ~250-word sample answers, collapsed until you tap.

02

Textbook pages

Each question cites the standard book and page. We do not host PDFs — open your own copy to the page.

03

MCQ practice

Rapid-fire tutor mode on the same lesson. Filter by topic when you want a longer drill.

04

Trackers

Custom lists of lessons for internals, plus a university exam window when dates are published.

05

Bookmarks

Save any question from review and come back to it later. Your list lives on your account, not just on one device.

06

No in-app store

The Android app never takes a card. When checkout is live, you pay on this site and refresh your profile in the app.

How it works

One sitting, then the debrief

Create an account in the app

Sign in with email. Every new account starts on the Free plan. We do not show ads.

Pick a paper or build a practice set

Locked Pro and Elite tests stay visible so you know what exists — you just cannot open the questions until you upgrade.

Sit it. Submit. Review.

Scoring is done on the server, not on your phone. Then open results and bookmark the questions that still bother you.

FAQ

Before you install

Is this a medical app or a clinical tool?

No. Medico is exam prep for KUHS MBBS university papers. It is not a medical device and it does not give clinical advice.

Do you take payment inside the Android app?

No. Google Play Billing is not used. Plans will be purchased on this website. The app only opens this site in your browser.

Who is it for?

Adults in MBBS at KUHS-affiliated colleges (18+). It is not directed at children.

Where is the app available?

Android first, via Google Play. The public listing will link here for privacy and account pages. iOS comes later.

Questions, privacy, or account requests

Play Store and App Store listings use the pages in the footer. Email support if you need a human.
