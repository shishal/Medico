Checkout

Plan upgrades will be paid on this page with the same email you use in the Medico app. The app never takes a card. That is the Play Store / App Store boundary we keep.

When this page is switched on you will:

  1. Sign in with your Medico email.
  2. Choose Pro (₹1,499 / 6 months) or Elite (₹2,999 / 12 months).
  3. Pay through Razorpay (UPI or card).
  4. Return to the app → Profile → refresh to see the new plan.

Until then, nothing on this site can charge you. Compare plans on the home page. If you already paid in error, use refunds & cancel.
