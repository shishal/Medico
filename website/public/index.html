What you can do

The loop that actually prepares you

Browse a paper, read the rules, sit it against the clock, then review score, accuracy, and every explanation your plan allows.

01

Catalog tests

Mini, Subject, Mock, and Grand. Grand tests follow the real NEET-PG shape: five timed sections with a section lock.

02

Live test engine

Question palette, mark-for-review, and a timer that auto-submits at zero. You see duration and marking before the clock starts.

03

Practice your way

Filter by subject, topic, and difficulty. Tutor mode shows the answer now; exam mode waits until the end.

04

Results that split the paper

Score, accuracy, percentile, and a subject-wise breakdown — so you know what to drill next.

05

Bookmarks

Save any question from review and come back to it later. Your list lives on your account, not just on one device.

06

No in-app store

The Android app never takes a card. When checkout is live, you pay on this site and refresh your profile in the app.

FAQ

Before you install

Is this a medical app or a clinical tool?

No. Medico is exam prep for NEET-PG. It is not a medical device and it does not give clinical advice.

Do you take payment inside the Android app?

No. Google Play Billing is not used. Plans will be purchased on this website. The app only opens this site in your browser.

Who is it for?

Adults preparing for NEET-PG (18+). It is not directed at children.

Where is the app available?

Android first, via Google Play. The public listing will link here for privacy and account pages. iOS comes later.

Questions, privacy, or account requests

Play Store and App Store listings use the pages in the footer. Email support if you need a human.
