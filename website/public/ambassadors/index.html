Become a Medico ambassador

College-specific internal trackers need someone on campus who can share exam windows (without breaking college rules). We pick generally one student per college per academic year.

What you do

  • Be the point of contact for your college batch.
  • Help classmates start the 4-day trial.
  • Share internal / university dates we can turn into a tracker.

What you get

  • Pro access for your ambassador term.
  • Direct line to the product team.

There is no CRM in the app yet — apply by emailing support@medico.shishal.com with your college, year, and KUHS register number.
